## 03/11 · Email is on the clock, costly and slow.
Infos: (1) the Reg E clock (2) cost and speed (3) the switching tax.
Script:
- An email is a written error notice under Reg E, so the clock starts the moment it lands: 10 business days to act, 45 days to resolve, and 90 for POS, foreign or first-30-day cases.

- Email is also the slowest and priciest channel the bank runs: 6 to 12 dollars a contact, 27 hours to a first reply, 22 percent of threads ending in "please call us".
- And 28 percent of agent time goes to switching systems and re-typing, not to the customer.

## 06/11 · The Policy RIL Matrix governs autonomy.
Infos: (1) R×I → L (2) locked cells (3) the never list (4) graduation.
Script:
- Every cell is a product of two: the category sets the row, the identity level of the sender sets the column, and R times I gives L, where the human sits.
- Where identity is missing, servicing and transactions are locked: the reply is a locked template that carries no account data, and step-up opens the rest.
- Complaints have no road on email at all, because the tool functions do not exist on this channel, and disputes stay L1, human-signed, permanently.
- Nothing starts autonomous: backtest, shadow, then dual sign-off by compliance and operations, with auto-demotion on drift. Graduation never unlocks a locked cell.
Captions:
- R × I = L: the category sets the row, identity sets the column.
- Locked cells reply with a locked template; step-up opens the rest.
- Complaints have no road on email; disputes stay human-signed.

## 11/11 · Success metrics.
Infos: (1) VARR (2) median TTR (3) the veto (4) the guardrails and the ROI ceiling.
Script:
- The headline is the verified autonomous resolution rate: 25 to 35 percent by six months, where resolved means no human touch and no reopen within 14 days, and the number has to survive QA sampling.
- Time to resolution, not to first reply, measured from the arrival timestamp: under 15 minutes when it runs autonomously, under a day otherwise, disputes excepted, against a 3.2-day baseline that is still an assumption.
- And a veto stands over it: one critical error voids the whole rate. A key error is a wrong action, a wrong party, a missed clock or a broken promise, so the bar is zero key errors, clocks at 100 percent, and recall of at least 99.5 percent. Non-negotiable.
- Twelve guardrails run alongside, and the 35 percent ROI base case is the top of this band, at steady state.
