04 Scope

Sort by category first, then automate.

Sort order first: volume × determinability × who they are, gated by penalty risk. Then one line: everything reversible goes to AI, everything consequential stays with a human.

Human-only in phase 1: complaints, fraud (the customer says they were defrauded) and off-brief mail are detected, clocked and handed to a person. Spam / noise is auto-archived in Phase 1 and never enters intake.

08 Self-service tool chain

How an email becomes a case — and how new intents enter the catalog.

One pipeline reads every inbound email; one config-driven loop adds new intents without retraining the model.

Each hit carries evidence sentences + confidence. Regulated intents start their clock at intake; adjudication stays human-only.

Bank admin (customer)
1.Sees a new intent surface in the review queue (unknown / low-confidence).
2.Names it, adds a short description, examples and keywords via config UI.
3.Sets routing, SLA, reply template and identity threshold.
4.Approves → intent is live in the catalog, no model retrain.
Vendor (us)
1.Zero-shot / clustering surfaces candidate new intents across banks.
2.Aggregates and anonymizes them; validates with backtest.
3.Batches a single shared-model retrain; ships one upgrade to all banks.
4.No per-bank model training; support stays scalable.

Backup · B4 Trade-offs

Reversible earns it. Irreversible never does.

We choose
The price we accept
Default L2, graduate one intent at a time
Every write starts behind a human one-click and only moves to L3 with backtest evidence and a dual signature.
↔
A slower first month
We ship fewer autonomous actions than a rival promising 90% on day one — and compliance signs the same sheet as the business.
Dispute intake only — never adjudication
AI files, clocks, receipts and chases; investigators decide, AI drafts the outcome letter, a person signs it.

The biggest line item on the table
We give up the full-dispute automation prize and keep the license to operate — R4 cells have no graduation path at all.

Also given up, on purpose: assembled letters over fluent ones, an in-thread step-up link over in-app, a runtime of our own, and mega-bank deals — they build, and Sierra owns that story.

Backup · B5 Risk

The risks that would end this product.

RISK 01
Missing a regulated intent
Half a sentence is a running clock.
136 violations74% sat in error resolution (2025)
What we built
  • High-recall detection at ingest, before an agent opens the mail
  • False positives preferred — recall ≥99.5% gates all autonomy
  • No samples in 90 days means no L3
RISK 02
The email attack surface
BEC, ATO and attachment injection — under ATO every normal identity signal still passes.
BECATOattachment injection
quarantined as data⊘R3 has no tool
What we built
  • Injected text is quarantined as data, never as instruction
  • R3 combinations have no callable function on this channel
  • Confirmation goes only to on-file contacts
RISK 03
Numeric hallucination
An invented fee or date is a Reg E and UDAAP violation; one wrong write can be unrecoverable.
✓number from a system slot → sent
✕model-written number → locked template
✎money stays R4 · L1 human-signed
What we built
  • Numbers only from system slots — never written by the model
  • Failed claim check → the locked template
  • Money stays R4: L1 human-signed

Also on the register: tipping-off vs duty to speak (31 CFR 1020.320(e)) · over-disclosure to an unverified sender (GLBA) · third-party risk review · vulnerable-customer fairness · the LLM-as-judge flipping verdicts in evaluation (14%) · sign-off fatigue — L2 approvals and AI nominations both drift toward rubber-stamping (watch the approve-without-edit rate, sample the approved files) · a step-up link that collides with “never click a link in an email” training.
