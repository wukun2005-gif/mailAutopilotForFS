2 · 修法：在服务端先判定语言，再把它写进 System 段

The autonomy rate has three conditions attached. Which of the three is most likely to be gamed by a customer who wants the number to look good?
The one most exposed to gaming is the **recall threshold on regulated-intent detection** — the ≥99.5% "one miss = one violation" bar. A team could tune the detector to over-flag (high recall, low precision), which inflates the "caught everything" story while quietly depressing the autonomy rate and ROI; the trade-off register explicitly accepts this "宁可误报多升级" to protect the compliance signature [1]. The graduation-sample count (n≥600) is harder to game because it's a statistical artifact of rule of three — you can't fake the sample size, only wait [2]. The action-whitelist restriction (high-impact actions never enter the tool) isn't gameable at all: it's a hard architectural exclusion, not a metric [1].
